525 0.1 Windows x64 preview

1. Close your DAW.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Copy the entire 525.vst3 folder into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
4. Open your DAW and rescan VST3 plug-ins.

The header selects the 2520 or 990 audio amplifier profile. Enable Mod and
click the ratio label in Compress to cycle 2:1, 4:1, 8:1 and 12:1.
Use BYP for dry audio. OFF retains the modeled audio path with gain reduction off.

This preview uses vintage 525C SPICE characterization. Some control regions
and timing, phase and distortion conditions are still being verified.
